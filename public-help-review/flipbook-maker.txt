Your questions, answered

Good to knowCan I use images instead of a PDF?

Yes. The creator accepts PDF files and page images. Arrange the images in the order you want readers to see them.Can readers change my publication?

No. The public reader lets visitors read, zoom and share. The account owner manages content, layout and themes in the archive.Are PDF files processed locally?

Page preparation happens in your browser. Choosing to publish online uploads the prepared publication to VikingTools so your audience can open its link. Keep your original files as a separate backup.
