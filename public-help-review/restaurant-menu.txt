Your questions, answered

Good to knowDoes a restaurant need Stripe to receive orders?

No. Guests pay at the restaurant by default, so you can receive orders without Stripe. Online payment is optional and requires the restaurant’s payment connection. A VikingTools subscription does not connect restaurant payments automatically.Can customers browse without signing in?

Yes. Everyone can view the public menu. The restaurant decides whether ordering requires verified email or sign-in, or whether guest ordering is allowed.Will a demo order reach a real restaurant?

No. The example below uses local sample orders. It demonstrates the staff queue, collection board and customer receipt without sending an order or taking a payment.
